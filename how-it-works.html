How it works

01 · Sense

Camera

A camera behind the windshield looks for headlights, taillights and bright signs, and works out the angle to each one.

02 · Decide

Control unit

Each LED segment covers a fixed slice of angle. The unit picks the slices that overlap each object, plus a small safety margin.

03 · Shape

LED segments

Those segments switch off or dim, many times a second. The dark gap follows the other car as it moves across your view.
